Autonomous Technology

Autonomous Mining Systems — Planning to Production

Autonomous mining systems delivered from planning to production. We support OEMs and operators with project execution, machine integration and site-ready deployment across Cat® MineStar™ Command, Fleet, Terrain, Detect and Health environments.

Built for remote, semi-autonomous and fully autonomous operation, our team helps turn advanced mining technology into production-ready equipment that performs at scale.

Cloud-based, subscription-managed application platform enabling browser access to the MineStar ecosystem.

Our Facility

Heavy Equipment Rebuild Facility

A 90-acre, multi-million-dollar mining equipment facility built for large-scale rebuilds, testing and machine readiness. Located between Bowen and Proserpine, the site supports diggers, drills, dozers and heavy production equipment through refurbishment, maintenance, commissioning and pre-site validation.

This facility gives mining clients a serious regional capability: faster turnaround, controlled testing, trade-backed rebuilds and equipment delivered ready for the field.
