Our Facility

90-Acre Heavy Equipment Facility

A strategic regional facility supporting mining machinery rebuilds, equipment testing, maintenance and large-scale transport staging just off the Bruce Highway.

GRB's 90-acre facility between Bowen and Proserpine has been developed as a multi-million-dollar hub for heavy mining equipment and regional transport support. Built for scale, the site gives clients, suppliers and transport operators a secure regional base for preparing, maintaining, testing and moving major machinery across North Queensland.
